Context Garden provides codebase insight to agents over MCP, by mirroring repos, converting them to markdown, and then building a graph+vector store. Agents get detailed, code-based, answers to their queries that focus on the interaction between the modules, files, and functions, not just the content of any one note. 


1. Seamless connection - if im on my lan, i can connect agents to my MCP server. 
2. insight - im provided a webui that shows me the state of the server, do some basic housekeeping, and also allows me to craft my own queries. 
3. Extensibility - the codebase has enough modularity that I can copy paste the logic from this graph+vector store to another project without rewiring a birds nest of code. simple, clean interfaces for sensible responsibility demarcations.
